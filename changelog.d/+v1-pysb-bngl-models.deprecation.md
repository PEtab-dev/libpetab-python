PySB and BNGL models are only supported with PEtab v2.
`petab.v1.models.pysb_model`, `petab.v1.models.bngl_model`, and loading
PySB or BNGL models via `petab.v1` are deprecated and will be removed in a
future release; use `petab.v2.models.pysb_model` and
`petab.v2.models.bngl_model` instead
([#622](https://github.com/PEtab-dev/libpetab-python/pull/622))
